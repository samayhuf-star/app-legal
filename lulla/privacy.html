This policy describes how Lulla (“the app”) handles information. Lulla is a sleep-sounds app for iPhone and Android. It is published by Samay Vashsht.

The short version

Lulla plays ambient sleep sounds. It has no accounts and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or microphone audio. It does not record sound.

What stays on your device

Lulla stores, on your device, how many free listening seconds you have used (in the device keychain or keystore) and your sound and timer choices. This is used to enforce a free listening period of three minutes before the paywall. Deleting the app may not reset the free-minute count on iOS, because it is stored in the keychain.

Purchases

You can listen for three minutes for free. After that, Lulla Premium is required. Premium is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

The purchases offered are an auto-renewing yearly subscription (product ID lulla_premium_yearly) and an auto-renewing monthly subscription (product ID lulla_premium_monthly). Both belong to the “Lulla Premium” subscription group. Either one unlocks Premium. Prices are shown in the app at the time of purchase from the store’s own localized price, and are not fixed in the app code. You can restore a previous purchase from the paywall.

The app talks to Apple or Google to load those products, complete or restore a purchase, and check whether Premium is still active. Purchase tokens stay on the device for that entitlement check. The app does not create an account, does not send a name or email address, and does not use a third-party purchase or analytics SDK.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and it does not send purchase or entitlement data to a third-party service.

Network calls

Aside from the store purchase flow, the app does not call our own servers. It may open system screens (your mail app, or the store’s subscription settings) at your request. There is no account to sign in to.

Background audio

Lulla can keep playing when your screen is locked so sleep sounds and the timer can continue. It does not record audio.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Apple or Google processes the payment under their own policies. The app does not share purchase history or an account ID with us or with a third-party SDK.

Retention and deletion

On-device preferences are removed if you delete the app, except that the free-minute count may remain in the iOS keychain. Purchase records are held by the store for as long as needed to provide the purchase and to meet their legal obligations. Restoring a purchase from the store will unlock Premium again if the store still shows a valid purchase.

Children

Lulla is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
