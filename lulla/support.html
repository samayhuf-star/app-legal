How does Lulla work?

Lulla plays looping sleep sounds such as rain, ocean, wind, fireplace and white noise. You can layer several sounds, set each volume, and use a sleep timer with a gentle fade-out. Playback can continue with the screen off.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login.

What is free, and what is Lulla Premium?

Seven sounds are free forever, with no time limit. Lulla Premium unlocks the rest of the library. Premium is an auto-renewing yearly subscription (product ID lulla_premium_yearly, $19.99 a year in the US) or an auto-renewing monthly subscription (product ID lulla_premium_monthly, $2.99 a month in the US). Both include a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid being charged. Prices are shown in the app at the time of purchase from the store.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

On the paywall, tap Restore purchases. That checks your Apple ID or Google account for an existing Lulla Premium subscription and unlocks the app again if one is found. It does not charge you again. To cancel a subscription on iPhone, use Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Android subscriptions are managed in Google Play.

The sounds

Lulla has 43 sounds. The nature, rain, water and hum sounds are real field recordings released by their recordists under Creative Commons licences (CC0 or CC BY), mastered as seamless loops of up to five minutes. White, pink, green and brown noise, the Om drone and Delta Waves are generated in-house. The lullabies are public-domain melodies (Brahms, Schubert and a traditional tune) played in-house with the FluidR3 soundfont (MIT licence). Delta Waves is a binaural beat and only works with headphones.
