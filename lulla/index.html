Lulla is a sleep-sounds app for iPhone and Android. You can layer rain, ocean, wind, fireplace, white noise and other sounds, set a sleep timer, and keep playing with the screen off. Seven sounds are free forever, with no time limit. Lulla Premium unlocks the rest of the library, with a 7-day free trial on the $2.99 monthly and $19.99 yearly subscriptions.
