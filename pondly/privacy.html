Pondly is a water-tracking app for iPhone. This page explains what it does with information.

The short version

No account and no email. Your drink log stays on your device. If you choose to join a Circle with friends, only a first name and your daily goal percentage are shared with the people in that Circle.

Stored on your device

Your water log, goal, weight, activity level, wake and sleep times, streak and collected Pips are kept on your device only.

Location

If you allow it, Pondly reads your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) while you use the app, to look up today's high temperature from Open-Meteo and adjust your goal. Your location is not stored by us and is not linked to you. You can decline and pick a temperature manually.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device if you turn them on. They do not involve a server.

Circles (optional)

Circles let friends see each other's progress. When you create or join one, Pondly sends our Circles server: the display name you type, a random device token that identifies you inside the Circle, the Circle code, and your daily goal percentage (0 to 100). Pondly never sends how much you drank or when. Members of a Circle can see your display name and percentage, and can send you one nudge a day. Leaving a Circle removes your membership record. Do not use your full name. To report abuse or ask for your Circle data to be deleted, email us.

Purchases

Pondly Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the Apple App Store with a 7-day free trial; you can also restore purchases in the app. Payment is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status, not your name or email. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

Analytics and advertising

Pondly has no advertising and does not track you across other apps or sites.

Health information

Water intake is not sent off your device, except as the goal percentage described under Circles. Pondly does not use HealthKit.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes on-device data. Leaving a Circle removes your Circle record. Email us to request deletion of any server-side data.

Children

Pondly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Circles are intended for ages 13 and over.

Changes and contact
