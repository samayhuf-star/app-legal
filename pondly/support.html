Do I need an account?

No. Pondly works without an account.

How is my goal worked out?

From your weight and activity, plus extra for hot weather and workouts you log.

How do Circles work?

Start a Circle and share the 6-letter code, or enter a friend's code. Friends see your name and goal percentage only. Leave any time from the Circles tab.

How do I report someone or delete my Circle data?

Email us the Circle code and name. We will remove them or your data.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Use Restore on the paywall, or manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
