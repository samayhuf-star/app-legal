Ludo is a board game for iPhone. This page explains what it does with information.

What stays on your device

Game progress and settings, such as whether you finished the intro, are stored only on your device. Ludo has no account system and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Purchases

Ludo Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the Apple App Store with a 7-day free trial; you can restore purchases in the app. Payment is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status, not your name or email. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

Analytics and advertising

Ludo has no advertising and does not track you across other apps or sites.

Children

Ludo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
