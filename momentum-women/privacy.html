Momentum – Women's Workout

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

The short version

Momentum – Women's Workout is a training app for women who want guided sessions on iPhone, at home or in the gym. Workout history stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Optional Momentum Pro purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details and only receive Apple's confirmation that a purchase is active, checked on your device.

No account

You can use the app as a guest. There is no sign-in service and no cloud backup of your workouts.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your guest session, completed workouts, streak, and a local record of a Momentum Pro purchase in the app's storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes this information.

Health data

This version does not read from or write to Apple Health. The app does not estimate or store medical information, and it does not give medical, diet, or weight-loss advice.

Purchases

If you buy Momentum Pro, Apple processes the payment through StoreKit on your device. We do not use a third-party purchase SDK.

Children

Momentum – Women's Workout does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
