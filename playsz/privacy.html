This policy describes how Dimmer (“the app”) handles information. Dimmer is a private video vault and offline video player. It is published by Samay Vashsht.

What the app collects

Dimmer does not collect personal information on our servers. The app has no backend and no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service.

Playback position, settings, and a local record of whether you have unlocked Pro stay on your device. Those values are not uploaded to us.

To play videos you already have, the app reads your device’s video library. If you choose to move a video into the private vault, the app copies it into storage private to the app and, only after you confirm the system prompt, deletes the original from your Photos library. The vault is locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Encrypted backups are created only when you choose to export, are protected by a password you set, and are saved where you choose. The app does not upload your videos, photos, or files.

Purchases

Optional Dimmer Pro is sold through Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. Google or Apple processes the payment. The app stores an on-device entitlement after a successful purchase or restore. We do not receive your payment card details.

Permissions

The app asks for access to videos so it can play them. Face ID is used only to unlock your private vault, and the app never receives your biometric data. The app does not ask for location, camera, or contacts.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share your information with advertisers or analytics companies. Store purchases are handled by Google or Apple under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Because the app’s data lives on your device, you can clear it from the device (and from in-app Settings, where you can remove vault items). Restoring a purchase from the store will unlock Pro again if the store still shows a valid purchase. Uninstalling the app removes the local data.

Children

Dimmer is not directed at children and is not a children’s app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Camera, microphone and failed-unlock log

Dimmer's private camera uses the camera (and the microphone for video) only while the camera screen is open. Photos and videos you capture are stored inside the app's private vault on your device and are not added to your Photos library. When an unlock attempt fails, Dimmer stores the time of the attempt on your device so you can review it; no picture, location or other data is collected, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
