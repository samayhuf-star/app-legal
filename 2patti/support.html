How does 2 Patti work?

Everyone gets two cards. In Centre mode, one shared card opens after the first betting round and becomes everyone’s third card. In Imaginary mode there is no centre card, and each player’s third card is the best card for their own two cards. The best three-card hand wins. A-2-3 is the highest straight, and equal hands are decided by suits (Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs), so there is always one winner. The full rules are in the app under How to play.

Are the chips real money?

No. Chips are virtual, free, and have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, sold, or exchanged for anything.

I ran out of chips

Claim the daily bonus in the lobby, or use the free top-up that appears when your balance is below the table minimum.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your chips and settings stay on your device and are removed if you delete the app.

Who are the other players?

In this version every opponent is a computer player, marked BOT at the table.
