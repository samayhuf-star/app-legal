This policy describes how 2 Patti (“the app”) handles information. 2 Patti is a social card game for adults, published by Samay Vashsht.

The short version

The app does not collect personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app. You play against computer players on your device.

What stays on your device

Your virtual chip balance, daily bonus streak, the confirmation that you are 18 or older, and your settings (such as vibration) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or share your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or usage data.

Purchases

This version of the app has no in-app purchases. Virtual chips are free and have no cash value.

Network calls

The app works offline. It only opens a web page when you tap a link to this policy, the terms, or support.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any information, because the app does not collect any.

Children

2 Patti is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and the app asks you to confirm your age before you play.

Changes

If a later version adds features that handle personal data, such as online play or accounts, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
