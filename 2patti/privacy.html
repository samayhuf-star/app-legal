This policy describes how 2 Patti (“the app”) handles information. 2 Patti is a free social card game for people 17 and older, published by Samay Vashsht. Before you play, you confirm that you are 17 or older. That confirmation is a self-declaration. The app does not check your identity.

The short version

The app does not collect personal data and does not send data to servers. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app. You play against computer players on your device. Version 1.0 is free and uses virtual chips only.

What stays on your device

Your virtual chip balance, the confirmation that you are 17 or older, and your settings (such as vibration) stay only on your device. The app gives a flat 250-chip daily bonus and stores no streak. There is no account. No data is collected or sent to servers. Deleting the app deletes what is stored on the device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or share your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or usage data. Nothing from the app is sent to servers.

Virtual chips

Chips are free. They have no cash value and cannot be bought or cashed out. Your chip balance stays on your device.

Network calls

The app works offline. It only opens a web page when you tap a link to this policy, the terms, or support.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any information, because the app does not collect any.

Children

2 Patti is for people 17 and older. It is not directed at anyone under 17. Before you play, you confirm that you are 17 or older. That confirmation is a self-declaration. The app does not check your identity.

Changes

If a later version adds features that handle personal data, such as online play or accounts, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
