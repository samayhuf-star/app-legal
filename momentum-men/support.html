How does the app work?

Momentum – Men's Workout is a training app for men who want short strength sessions on iPhone. The beginner plan, the workout player (timer or reps, with on-screen form cues), and on-device progress tracking are free. Progress is stored on your device. There is no account.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login. Continue as Guest. Progress stays on this device.

What is free, and what is Momentum Pro?

The beginner plan, the workout player (timer or reps, with on-screen form cues), and on-device progress tracking are free. Optional Momentum Pro unlocks the remaining 30-day plans and the custom session builder. It is $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year, each with a 7-day free trial, or $39.99 once for life with no trial. Prices are shown in the app from the App Store at the time of purchase and may vary by country.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app, go to Home, then open Momentum Pro and tap Restore. You can also open the paywall from a locked plan. Restore checks your Apple ID for an existing Momentum Pro purchase and unlocks the app again if one is found. It does not charge you again.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Find Momentum – Men's Workout and cancel it there. You must cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or the current period to avoid renewal. Apple also explains how to cancel here: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428.
