Twist 21

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Twist 21 is a card game rated 17+, published by Samay Vashsht. Blackjack with action cards: hit and stand against two rivals, then play Skip, Swap, Peek, Shield and more to bend the table, and even the target number itself. Matches are scored with virtual points and chips only. Those points and chips have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, goods, or prizes. There is no real-money play.

Free to play: 3 matches a day and 5 action cards. Twist 21 Premium is an optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase (a lifetime unlock). In the United States the price is US$39.99, charged by Apple In-App Purchase through StoreKit at the price shown before you confirm. It unlocks all 9 action cards, unlimited matches, and the bust-chance meter. It is not a subscription. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.
