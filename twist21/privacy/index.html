Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Twist 21 is made by Samay Vashsht (samayhuf@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with information. The app collects no data and makes no network requests to third parties. It does not require an account. The in-app purchase is handled by Apple.

What stays on your device

Your match progress, including virtual points and chips, how many free matches you have played today, and a local note of whether Premium is unlocked are stored on your device (in the app's local storage). We do not run a game server and we cannot see that data. The app does not send it to anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Twist 21 Premium (product ID twist21_premium) is an optional one-time, non-consumable lifetime unlock via Apple In-App Purchase. It is not a subscription and it does not renew. The amount Apple charges is the price shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. In the United States that price is US$39.99. Apple processes the payment. No third-party payment processor is involved. We never receive your name, email, or payment card number.

Apple keeps the store transaction and handles restore on this device. We do not receive a copy of your payment details, and we do not send purchase data to anyone else.

What we do not collect

  • No personal data. The app collects no data.
  • No account, name, or email, unless you email us yourself.
  • No advertising, and no analytics or tracking.
  • No sale of data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health information.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Virtual points and chips

Twist 21 is a card game rated 17+. Points and chips are free virtual game scoring only. They exist only inside the game. They cannot be bought, and they have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods, or prizes. There is no real-money play and no real-money prize.

How long it is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app or clear its storage. Apple keeps the purchase record so a later restore through Apple In-App Purchase can succeed. We do not keep a separate copy.

Your choices

You can play the free game without buying. You can decline the purchase sheet. There is no account to delete inside the app, because the app does not create one. Questions: email samayhuf@gmail.com.

Children

Twist 21 is rated 17+ because it is a blackjack-style card game. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: Samay Vashsht, samayhuf@gmail.com.
