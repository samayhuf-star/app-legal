This policy describes how Chezz (“the app”) handles information. Chezz is a chess app for iPhone and Android. It is published by Samay Vashsht.

The short version

You can play as a guest or with an optional email account. Ranked results are verified by our server. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We do not show ads or use a third-party analytics or purchase SDK.

What we collect

  • Profile: you play as a guest or with an email account. A profile stores the display name, city, state and country you type in, plus your game statistics (points, wins, draws, losses, levels beaten, streaks, average move time, recent results). Display name, location and points appear on the public leaderboards. Do not enter personal information you do not want shown publicly.
  • Email account (optional): if you create an account we store your email address and a salted, hashed password (we never store or see your password in plain text). Your email is never shown to other players and is used only to sign you in.
  • Ranked games: to verify a ranked result we receive the list of moves of that game and a random game identifier.

What stays on your device

Your settings and any unfinished game are stored on your device. Your profile secret is kept in the device’s secure storage (keychain or keystore).

Purchases

Levels 1–10 are free. Chezz Pro unlocks levels 11–50 and unlimited hints. Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

The purchases offered are an auto-renewing yearly subscription (product ID chezz_pro_yearly) and an auto-renewing monthly subscription (product ID chezz_pro_monthly). Both belong to the “Chezz Pro” subscription group. Either one unlocks Pro. Prices are shown in the app at the time of purchase from the store’s own localized price, and are not fixed in the app code. You can restore a previous purchase from the paywall or from Profile.

The app talks to Apple or Google to load those products, complete or restore a purchase, and check whether Pro is still active. Purchase tokens stay on the device for that entitlement check. The app does not send a name or email address to the store, and it does not use a third-party purchase or analytics SDK.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and it does not send purchase or entitlement data to a third-party service.

Network calls

The app calls our game server to start ranked games, verify results, sign you in, update your profile, and load leaderboards. Aside from that and the store purchase flow, the app does not call other services. It may open system screens (your mail app, or the store’s subscription settings) at your request.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Display name, location text you entered, and points appear on public leaderboards. Apple or Google processes the payment under their own policies. The app does not share purchase history or an account ID with a third-party purchase SDK.

Retention and deletion

Profile, account and statistics are kept while your profile exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app: open Profile, then “Delete my account”. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also email samayhuf@gmail.com and quote your display name, and we will remove it. On-device settings are removed if you delete the app, except that a profile secret may remain in the iOS keychain until cleared. Purchase records are held by the store for as long as needed to provide the purchase and to meet their legal obligations. Restoring a purchase from the store will unlock Pro again if the store still shows a valid purchase.

Children

Chezz is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
