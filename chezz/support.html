How does Chezz work?

Chezz is a chess app with 50 bot levels, real clocks, and leaderboards. Levels 1–10 are free. Chezz Pro unlocks levels 11–50 and unlimited hints.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest. An optional email account lets you sign back in on another device and keeps your stats. Guest stats are stored on the device and can be lost if you sign out or delete the app.

What is free, and what is Chezz Pro?

Levels 1–10 and basic play are free. Chezz Pro is an auto-renewing yearly subscription (product ID chezz_pro_yearly) or an auto-renewing monthly subscription (product ID chezz_pro_monthly). Prices are shown in the app at the time of purchase from the store.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

On the paywall or in Profile, tap Restore purchases. That checks your Apple ID or Google account for an existing Chezz Pro subscription and unlocks the app again if one is found. It does not charge you again. To cancel a subscription on iPhone, use Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Android subscriptions are managed in Google Play.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile, then tap “Delete my account” and confirm. You can also email samayhuf@gmail.com and quote your display name.
