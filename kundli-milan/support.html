How does Kundli Milan work?

Enter your date, time and place of birth to see your kundli and a colour-coded month-by-month and year-by-year outlook. Add a second person to get a match-making report with the Ashtakoot Gun Milan score out of 36. You can save a report as a PDF and share it.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login. Your birth details stay on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a profile inside the app, or delete the app. Everything you entered is stored only on your device. Questions about data tied to your anonymous purchase ID can be sent to the email above.

Subscription, restore and cancel

Kundli Milan Premium is $19.99 a year or $2.99 a month in the US, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers; prices are shown in the app at purchase. To restore, tap Restore purchases on the paywall. To cancel, use Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Why is my chart different from another app?

Kundli Milan uses the Lahiri ayanamsa and Vimshottari dashas. Other apps may use different settings, and birth time or place differences change results.
