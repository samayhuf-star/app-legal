Time Kundli is an iPhone app that builds your kundli (birth chart) from your birth date, time and place. It shows a month-by-month and year-by-year outlook as colour-coded blocks, from calm to cautious to danger periods, and creates a match-making (Ashtakoot Gun Milan) report for two people. The app is available in English and Hindi.

Time Kundli Premium is an auto-renewing subscription: $1.99 a week in the US, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the app at purchase from the store and may vary by country. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid being charged.
