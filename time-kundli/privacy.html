This policy describes how Time Kundli (“the app”) handles information. It is published by Samay Vashsht.

The short version

Time Kundli has no accounts. The birth details you enter are used to calculate your chart on your device and are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive them, and we do not sell your information.

What you enter

To build a kundli or a match-making report you enter a name or nickname, date of birth, time of birth and place of birth, for yourself and, for matching, for a second person. Charts, outlooks and match scores are calculated on your device. This information is saved locally so you can reopen your reports, and it is removed when you delete the profile in the app or delete the app.

Please enter another person’s details only if they are happy for you to do so.

Purchases

Time Kundli Premium is sold through the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Subscriptions are handled by Apple, and the purchase and entitlement status is managed through RevenueCat, a subscription service provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated for your install, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information needed to validate purchases. It does not receive your birth details, and the app does not send a name or email address to it. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not ask for permission to track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing reports

If you export or share a report as a PDF, it is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (for example Messages or Files). We do not receive a copy.

Retention and deletion

There is no account to delete. All birth details and reports are stored on your device only; delete a profile in the app or delete the app to remove them. Purchase records are held by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the purchase and meet legal obligations. To ask about data connected to your anonymous purchase ID, email samayhuf@gmail.com.

Entertainment, not advice

Astrology content is for reflection and entertainment. It is not medical, legal, financial or relationship advice.

Children

Time Kundli is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
