How do I scan a QR code or barcode?

Open Scanwise and point the camera at the code. You can also tap the photo button and choose an image from your library. Scanning runs on your device. Nothing you scan is uploaded.

What is Safe-Link Shield?

Safe-Link Shield checks every scanned link on your device before you open it. It can flag look-alike sites, shortened links, raw IP addresses and risky downloads, and it shows a Safe, Caution or Danger verdict with reasons. The check is not a guarantee of safety. You decide whether to open the link.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Scanwise Pro paywall and tap Restore purchases, or go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. That checks your Apple ID for an existing Scanwise Pro subscription and unlocks Pro again if one is found. It does not charge you again.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Find Scanwise and cancel it there. You must cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or the current period to avoid renewal. Apple also explains how to cancel here: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428.
