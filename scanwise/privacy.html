Scanwise

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version

Scanwise reads QR codes and barcodes on your device. Nothing you scan is uploaded to us. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Optional Scanwise Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details and only receive Apple's confirmation that a subscription is active, checked on your device.

Camera and photos

With your permission, Scanwise uses the camera only to read QR codes and barcodes. It does not record or upload camera video. If you choose a photo from your library, Scanwise reads codes from that photo only. Nothing from the photo is uploaded to us.

What is stored on your device

If history is enabled (it is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings), Scanwise saves recent scans and codes you mark as favourites in on-device storage, along with small preferences such as haptics and beep. You can delete individual items or clear history in the app. Deleting the app removes this information.

Codes you create

You can generate QR codes in the app. Those codes are created on your device.

Links you choose to open

If a code contains a link or other action and you choose to open it, your device may open a browser or another app. Scanwise can show on-device safety hints about a link (for example whether it uses https). That check runs on your device. We do not visit the link on your behalf.

Children

Scanwise does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
