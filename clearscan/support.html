How does ClearScan work?

ClearScan is a document scanner for iPhone. It reads text on your device, so you can search every page, and it can export a searchable PDF with no watermark. Scans are not uploaded. There is no account.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login.

What is free, and what is ClearScan Pro?

Scanning, text recognition, search, PDF export and app lock are free. Optional ClearScan Pro unlocks auto-sort and Word or Markdown export. It is $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year, each with a 7-day free trial, or $39.99 once for life with no trial. Prices are shown in the app from the App Store at the time of purchase and may vary by country.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open ClearScan, tap the settings icon, then tap Restore purchases. You can also open the ClearScan Pro paywall and tap Restore. That checks your Apple ID for an existing ClearScan Pro purchase and unlocks the app again if one is found. It does not charge you again.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Find ClearScan and cancel it there. You must cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or the current period to avoid renewal. Apple also explains how to cancel here: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428.
