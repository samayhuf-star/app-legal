Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

The short version

ClearScan scans documents and reads their text on your device. Nothing you scan is uploaded to us. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Optional ClearScan Pro purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details and only receive Apple's confirmation that a purchase is active, checked on your device.

Camera and photos

With your permission, ClearScan uses the camera only to scan documents you choose. If you choose photos from your library, ClearScan turns those photos into a document on your device. Images stay on your iPhone. Nothing from the camera or from a photo is uploaded to us.

Text recognition

Text recognition runs on your iPhone. It does not require an internet connection, and images and extracted text are not sent to us.

What is stored on your device

ClearScan saves your scans, the text read from them, exported files, and small preferences such as app lock in the app's storage on your iPhone. You can delete documents in the app. Deleting the app removes this information.

App lock

You can optionally lock the app with Face ID or your device passcode. That check is handled by iOS. We never see your biometric data.

Sharing

Files leave the app only when you choose to share or export them, for example as a PDF.

Purchases

If you buy ClearScan Pro, Apple processes the payment through StoreKit on your device. We do not use a third-party purchase SDK. Apple never receives your scans from us.

Children

ClearScan does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
