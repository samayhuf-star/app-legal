This policy describes how Klenzer (“the app”) handles information. Klenzer is a phone storage cleaner for iPhone and Android, published by Samay Vashsht.

The short version

Your photos, videos and contacts stay on your device and are never uploaded. There is no account and no sign-in. Klenzer does not use any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or third-party purchase SDK. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play on your device. Klenzer does not collect a name, email address, payment card details, device identifier or account ID.

Photos and videos

With your permission, Klenzer reads your photo and video library on the device to find duplicate and near-identical photos, blurry photos, screenshots, Live Photos, short and screen recordings, and large videos, and to measure their file sizes. It also reads the device’s total and free storage to show those figures on the home screen. This media is processed on the device only and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

When you convert a Live Photo to a still photo, Klenzer copies the original photo’s existing date, location, favourite state and album membership onto the new still. That location is the metadata already saved in the photo. Klenzer does not access your current location or use location services.

Swipe to Organize shows your photos month by month so you can mark photos to keep or delete. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Klenzer deletes or converts only the items you select, and only after your phone’s own confirmation prompt. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for up to 30 days. On Android, items you confirm are deleted by the system and Klenzer cannot restore them.

Contacts

Contacts access is optional and is requested only when you open Merge Contacts. If you allow it, Klenzer reads the names, phone numbers and email addresses in your address book on the device to find likely duplicates. When you confirm a merge, Klenzer copies any missing phone numbers and email addresses onto the contact that stays, then deletes the duplicate contacts from your address book. Other fields on the duplicates, such as addresses, birthdays, notes and photos, are not copied. Contacts are never uploaded, copied off the device, or stored by Klenzer outside your address book.

Secret Album and Face ID

Secret Album holds photos only. Photos you move into Secret Album are encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM and stored only inside Klenzer’s private storage on that device. They are not uploaded or backed up to us. The encryption key is created on your phone and kept in the system Keychain (Android Keystore on Android), for this device only. Your PIN is never stored, only a salted hash of it, also in the Keychain.

Face ID is optional and is used only to unlock Secret Album. iOS checks your face and tells Klenzer only whether it matched. Klenzer never receives or stores face data. After a photo is added to Secret Album, Klenzer can delete the original from Photos if you confirm. Klenzer cannot recover a forgotten PIN, and deleting the app deletes Secret Album and the photos in it.

Free daily limit

Scanning and reviewing your library is free. Without Klenzer Pro you get 10 free items each day, shared across deleting photos and videos, merging contacts and adding photos to Secret Album. This daily count is kept on your device only and is not sent anywhere.

What is stored on your device

Klenzer stores a few small preferences locally: whether you have finished the intro, how many free items you have used today, the running total of space you have cleared, whether you turned reminders on, and whether you chose not to see the Recently Deleted reminder again. It also keeps an on-device scan cache (file sizes and small image fingerprints, keyed by photo ID and modification date) so later scans can skip unchanged items, plus temporary thumbnails you can clear in the app. None of this is uploaded. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, Klenzer schedules a local notification on the device every few days with your current used and total storage. Reminders are created and delivered by your phone itself. No server is involved, nothing is sent to us, and no push token is collected. You can turn reminders off in Klenzer Settings or in your device Settings.

Purchases

Beyond the free daily limit, deleting items, merging contacts and adding photos to Secret Album require Klenzer Pro. Converting Live Photos to stills requires Klenzer Pro. Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play as an auto-renewing yearly subscription (com.krosz.klenzer.yearly), an auto-renewing monthly subscription (com.krosz.klenzer.monthly), or a one-time lifetime unlock (com.krosz.klenzer.lifetime). Prices are shown in the app from the store’s own localized price at the time of purchase.

The app talks only to Apple or Google, using the store’s own purchase system on the device, to load products, complete or restore a purchase, and check whether Pro is active. We do not receive your payment card details, and no purchase data is sent to us or to any third-party service. You can restore a previous purchase from Settings or from the Klenzer Pro screen.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Klenzer has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. It does not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, attribution tokens or campaign attributes.

Permissions

Photo and video library access is needed to scan, delete, convert and add to Secret Album. Contacts access is optional and only requested when you open Merge Contacts. Face ID is optional and only used to unlock Secret Album. Notification permission is optional and only requested if you turn on reminders. Klenzer does not request camera, microphone, location or tracking permission. You can change or revoke any permission at any time in your device Settings.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Your photos, videos and contacts are not shared with anyone. Apple or Google processes payments under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Everything Klenzer stores, including Secret Album, stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google. Restoring a purchase unlocks Pro again if the store still shows a valid purchase.

Children

Klenzer is not directed at children under 13.
