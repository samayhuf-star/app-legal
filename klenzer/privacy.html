This policy describes how Klenzer (“the app”) handles information. Klenzer is a photo-library cleaner for iPhone and Android. It is published by Samay Vashsht.

Photos and videos stay on your device

With your permission, Klenzer reads your photo and video library on the device to find duplicates and near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos, and large videos, and to measure file sizes. It also reads the device’s total and free storage to show those figures on the home screen.

That media is processed on the device only. Klenzer does not upload your photos or videos to us or to anyone else. Scan results are kept in memory on the device and are discarded when you close the app. When the app looks up extra details about an item, it asks the system not to download the file from iCloud or the network.

Klenzer deletes only the items you select, and only after the device’s own confirmation prompt. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for a limited time; the space is freed once that album is emptied. The app cannot empty Recently Deleted for you. It can show how much is waiting there and open the Photos app so you can do it. On Android, items you confirm are deleted by the system, and Klenzer cannot restore them.

What is stored on your device

Klenzer stores a few small preferences locally: whether you have finished the intro, the running total of space and items you have cleared, whether you turned reminders on, and (on iPhone) how much space is still waiting in Recently Deleted. It also keeps temporary files such as image thumbnails, which you can clear from the home screen. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, Klenzer schedules a few occasional local notifications on the device (at most five over about six weeks, counted from the last time you open the app, plus one extra reminder the next day if space is still waiting in Recently Deleted). They are created and delivered by the device itself. No server is involved, nothing is sent to us for this feature, and no push-notification token is collected. You can turn reminders off in Klenzer Settings or in the device Settings.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Scanning and reviewing your library is free. Deleting items and clearing Klenzer’s temporary files requires Klenzer Pro. Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play and is processed with RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). We do not receive your payment card details.

The purchases offered are a one-time lifetime unlock (product ID com.krosz.klenzer.lifetime) and an auto-renewing monthly subscription (product ID com.krosz.klenzer.monthly). Both unlock the same entitlement, klenzer_pro. Prices are shown in the app at the time of purchase and are not fixed in the app code. You can restore a previous purchase from Settings.

When the purchase system is configured, the app contacts RevenueCat to load offerings, complete or restore a purchase, and check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives:

  • an app user ID created by RevenueCat (the app does not create an account or send a name or email address; it does not pass its own user ID to RevenueCat);
  • purchase history and entitlement status for the products above;
  • subscriber attributes the app sets: app name, app version, build number, locale, operating-system version, and platform;
  • the source and time of a paywall being shown, and a one-time “first clean” milestone date if you complete a clean;
  • device identifiers collected by the RevenueCat SDK, and, on iPhone, an Apple Search Ads (AdServices) attribution token;
  • campaign attributes if the app is opened from a link that includes standard utm_ or ref parameters.

RevenueCat also records paywall impressions, closes, and conversions when a RevenueCat paywall is shown. Offerings, product configuration, and the paywall design are loaded from RevenueCat. RevenueCat’s handling of this information is described in its own privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The only third-party service that receives the usage and identifier data listed above is RevenueCat, as part of purchases, entitlements, and the attribution features the SDK is configured to use.

Network calls

Aside from RevenueCat and the store purchase flow, the app does not call our own servers. It may open system screens (Settings, Photos, your mail app, or the store’s review prompt) at your request. There is no account to sign in to.

Permissions

Photo and video library access is required to scan and delete items. Notification permission is optional and is requested only if you turn on reminders. The app does not ask for location, camera, contacts, or tracking permission (it has no App Tracking Transparency prompt).

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Your photos and videos are not shared. Purchase and the related identifier and attribution data described above are sent to RevenueCat so Pro can be sold, restored, and managed. Apple or Google processes the payment under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

On-device preferences and temporary files are removed if you delete the app. Purchase records and the RevenueCat app user ID are held by RevenueCat and the store for as long as needed to provide the purchase and to meet their legal obligations. Restoring a purchase from the store will unlock Pro again if the store still shows a valid purchase.

Children

Klenzer is not directed at children under 13.
