The app

Klenzer is a phone storage cleaner. It finds duplicate and near-identical photos, blurry photos, screenshots, Live Photos, short and screen recordings, and large videos on your device and lets you delete the items you choose. It also offers Swipe to Organize, Merge Contacts (which copies missing phone numbers and email addresses onto one contact and removes the duplicates) and Secret Album (an encrypted, photos-only album stored on the device and unlocked with Face ID or a PIN). It does not clear other apps’ data or system files, and it cannot empty Recently Deleted on iPhone for you.

Nothing is deleted automatically. You review items and confirm each deletion with the system prompt. You are responsible for the items you choose to delete.

Auto-renewing subscriptions (Apple)

If you buy a yearly or monthly subscription on an iPhone, payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
