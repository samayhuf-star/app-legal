How does Klenzer work?

Klenzer scans your photo and video library on the device to find duplicates and near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos, and large videos. You review what it finds and choose what to delete. Nothing is deleted until you confirm the system prompt. Your photos are not uploaded.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login.

What is free, and what is Klenzer Pro?

Scanning and reviewing your library is free. Deleting selected items and clearing Klenzer’s temporary files requires Klenzer Pro. Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock (product ID com.krosz.klenzer.lifetime) or an auto-renewing monthly subscription (product ID com.krosz.klenzer.monthly). Prices are shown in the app at the time of purchase.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

In Settings, tap Restore purchases. That checks your Apple ID or Google account for an existing Klenzer Pro purchase and unlocks the app again if one is found. It does not charge you again. To cancel a monthly subscription on iPhone, use Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or tap Manage Klenzer Pro in the app after Pro is unlocked. Android subscriptions are managed in Google Play.

Why does Klenzer need photo access?

It has to read your library to find and measure items, and to delete the ones you confirm. You can allow full access or selected photos. Without photo access, the app will open but cannot scan. You can change this later in the device Settings.

What are cleanup reminders?

They are optional. If you turn them on and grant notification permission, the app schedules a few local notifications on the device (at most five over about six weeks, restarted when you open the app). No push server is used. You can turn them off in Klenzer Settings or in the device Settings.

I deleted items on iPhone but the space did not come back.

On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. Klenzer cannot empty that album. Open Photos and empty Recently Deleted to free the space. Klenzer can show how much is waiting and offer a shortcut to Photos.
